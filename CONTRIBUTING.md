# Bring your curiosity

Choose a project and read its README first. It describes what the project does, how to try it, and which checks matter.

For a bug, tell us what you tried, what you expected, and what happened. For an idea, explain who it helps and give one concrete example.

Keep your first change small enough to explain. Open a draft pull request early if you want help thinking it through. Say which checks you ran and which remain pending.

Phone-based contributions are welcome. You can draft text, propose code or tests, and respond to review. A collaborator can run checks on your exact commit. Changes after that check need another run.

Keep credentials and personal information out of public posts and commits. Use the project's existing collaborator spaces for private work. Credit the people and projects your work builds on.

Make room for someone else's first project. Explain the thing you wish someone had explained to you. Questions are part of the work.
